## Key Idea: Interior and Exterior Angles

An **interior angle** is inside a polygon at a vertex.

An **exterior angle** is formed when one side of the polygon is extended past a vertex. The exterior angle sits outside the polygon.

When an exterior angle is next to its interior angle, they form a **linear pair**.

That means:

`interior angle + adjacent exterior angle = 180 degrees`

If the interior angle is `i` and the adjacent exterior angle is `e`, then:

`i + e = 180`

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The word adjacent matters. This lesson compares an exterior angle with the interior angle beside it at the same vertex.

## Worked Example

A polygon has an interior angle of 125 degrees. One side is extended to form an adjacent exterior angle. Find the exterior angle.

Set up the relationship:

`interior angle + exterior angle = 180`

Substitute the known measure:

`125 + e = 180`

Solve:

`e = 180 - 125`

`e = 55`

The adjacent exterior angle measures 55 degrees.

## Why This Works

The interior angle and its adjacent exterior angle share:

- the same vertex
- one side of the polygon
- a straight line made by the original side and its extension

Because the outside ray continues the side in a straight line, the two angles fill a straight angle.

So they are supplementary.

## Scaffolded Task

Complete the table. Use `interior + exterior = 180`.

| Interior angle | Equation | Exterior angle |
|---:|---|---:|
| 90 degrees | `90 + e = 180` | 90 degrees |
| 110 degrees | `110 + e = 180` | ___ |
| 72 degrees | `72 + e = 180` | ___ |
| ___ | `i + 48 = 180` | 48 degrees |

Answers:

- `110 + e = 180`, so `e = 70`
- `72 + e = 180`, so `e = 108`
- `i + 48 = 180`, so `i = 132`

## Writing Angle Equations

Sometimes the missing measure is written as a variable.

Example: An adjacent exterior angle is 64 degrees. The interior angle is `x`.

Write:

`x + 64 = 180`

Solve:

`x = 116`

The interior angle is 116 degrees.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not set adjacent interior and exterior angles equal unless the problem gives a special reason. In general, they add to 180 degrees.

## Practice Items

Solve each item in your notebook. Show an equation before calculating.

1. An interior angle is 140 degrees. Find the adjacent exterior angle.
2. An exterior angle is 35 degrees. Find the adjacent interior angle.
3. Write an equation for an interior angle `x` and an adjacent exterior angle of 82 degrees. Then solve for `x`.
4. A student says an exterior angle of 120 degrees must be next to an interior angle of 120 degrees. Is the student correct? Explain.
5. At one vertex of a polygon, the interior angle is twice the adjacent exterior angle. Find both angle measures.

Answers:

1. `140 + e = 180`, so `e = 40 degrees`.
2. `i + 35 = 180`, so `i = 145 degrees`.
3. `x + 82 = 180`, so `x = 98 degrees`.
4. No. Adjacent interior and exterior angles form a straight line, so they add to 180 degrees. The interior angle would be 60 degrees.
5. Let the exterior angle be `x`. Then the interior angle is `2x`. Write `2x + x = 180`, so `3x = 180` and `x = 60`. The exterior angle is 60 degrees and the interior angle is 120 degrees.
