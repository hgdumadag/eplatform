## Session 1: Circles and Similarity

> [!TARGET] Session Target
>
> **Target time:** 45-60 minutes
>
> Reconnect formulas with the geometry they describe.

### Warm-Up Recall

1. Circumference of a circle
2. Area of a circle
3. Ratio of corresponding sides in similar figures

### Circle Essentials

- Circumference: $C = 2\pi r$
- Area: $A = \pi r^2$
- Arc length uses the same fraction of the circumference as the central angle uses of $360^\circ$

**Example 1:**  
A circle has radius 7 cm. Find its circumference and area.

$$C = 2\pi(7) = 14\pi$$

$$A = \pi(7)^2 = 49\pi$$

### Similarity Essentials

If two triangles are similar, corresponding side lengths are in proportion.

**Example 2:**  
Two similar triangles have side lengths 6 and 15 on corresponding sides. If a side in the smaller triangle is 10, what is the corresponding side in the larger triangle?

Scale factor:

$$\frac{15}{6} = \frac{5}{2}$$

Corresponding larger side:

$$10 \cdot \frac{5}{2} = 25$$

## Session 2: Coordinate Geometry Refresher

> [!TARGET] Session Target
>
> **Target time:** 45-60 minutes
>
> Keep signs, subtraction order, and formula choice under control.

### Core Formulas

- Midpoint:

$$M = \left(\frac{x_1+x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1+y_2}{2}\right)$$

- Distance:

$$d = \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_2-y_1)^2}$$

- Slope:

$$m = \frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}$$

**Example 3:**  
Find the midpoint of $(2, -1)$ and $(8, 5)$.

$$M = \left(\frac{2+8}{2}, \frac{-1+5}{2}\right) = (5, 2)$$

**Example 4:**  
Find the slope through $(-3, 4)$ and $(1, -2)$.

$$m = \frac{-2-4}{1-(-3)} = \frac{-6}{4} = -\frac{3}{2}$$

> [!TIP] Session 2 Reminders
>
> - Similarity scales length by $k$, perimeter by $k$, and area by $k^2$.
> - A vertical line has undefined slope.
> - Always subtract in the same point order.

## Session 3: Mixed Geometry and Coordinate Checkpoint

> [!TARGET] Session Target
>
> **Target time:** 45 minutes
>
> Practice switching between figure-based and algebra-based reasoning.

### Mini Drill

1. Find the distance between $(0, 0)$ and $(6, 8)$.
2. A sector has central angle $90^\circ$ in a circle of radius 12. What fraction of the full circle is it?
3. Two similar figures have side ratio $2:5$. What is the area ratio?

### Final Worked Example

**Example 5:**  
Line A has slope 2. A line perpendicular to it passes through $(3, -1)$. Find the equation in point-slope form.

Perpendicular slope:

$$m = -\frac{1}{2}$$

Point-slope form:

$$y - (-1) = -\frac{1}{2}(x - 3)$$

$$y + 1 = -\frac{1}{2}(x - 3)$$

## Key Takeaways

- Similarity is about corresponding parts, not random sides.
- Coordinate geometry is just geometry written in algebra form.
- Keep subtraction order consistent and signs under control.
