## Main Concept Explanation

DNA, genes, and chromosomes are related levels of biological organization. A gene is a section of DNA, and a chromosome is a long coiled DNA molecule containing many genes.

The important study move is to connect the vocabulary to a clear relationship. Ask yourself: **What parts are involved? What changes or stays the same? What evidence would show that the idea is correct?**

> [!IMPORTANT] Key Idea Box
>
> DNA, genes, and chromosomes are related levels of biological organization. A gene is a section of DNA, and a chromosome is a long coiled DNA molecule containing many genes. For this Grade 9 Biology lesson, a strong answer uses correct vocabulary, a labeled diagram, and a relevant example.

## Misconception Alerts

- A gene is not larger than a chromosome; a chromosome contains many genes.
- A trait is not always controlled by only one gene; many traits involve several genes and the environment.
