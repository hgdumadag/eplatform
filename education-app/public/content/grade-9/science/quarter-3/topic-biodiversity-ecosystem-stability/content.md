## Main Concept Explanation

Biodiversity is the variety of living things in an ecosystem. High biodiversity can make an ecosystem more stable because different species can fill different roles and some may survive disturbances.

The important study move is to connect the vocabulary to a clear relationship. Ask yourself: **What parts are involved? What changes or stays the same? What evidence would show that the idea is correct?**

> [!IMPORTANT] Key Idea Box
>
> Biodiversity is the variety of living things in an ecosystem. High biodiversity can make an ecosystem more stable because different species can fill different roles and some may survive disturbances. ## Misconception Alerts

- Stability does not mean nothing changes; it means the ecosystem can keep functioning.
- High biodiversity does not make an ecosystem indestructible.
