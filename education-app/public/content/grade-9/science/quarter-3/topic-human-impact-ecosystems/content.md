## Main Concept Explanation

Human activities such as deforestation, pollution, and introduction of invasive species can reduce biodiversity, disrupt food webs, and damage habitats.

The important study move is to connect the vocabulary to a clear relationship. Ask yourself: **What parts are involved? What changes or stays the same? What evidence would show that the idea is correct?**

> [!IMPORTANT] Key Idea Box
>
> Human activities such as deforestation, pollution, and introduction of invasive species can reduce biodiversity, disrupt food webs, and damage habitats. For this Grade 9 Biology lesson, a strong answer uses correct vocabulary, a labeled diagram, and a relevant example.

### How to reason through an example

1. Identify the biological level: molecule, cell, organism, population, community, or ecosystem.
2. Identify the key parts or causes.
3. Use a diagram or table to show the relationship.
4. Explain the result using evidence from the situation.

## Misconception Alerts

- Human impact is not only direct killing; changing habitat can be just as damaging.
- Not every introduced species becomes invasive, but harmful introduced species need careful management.
