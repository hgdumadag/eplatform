## Key Vocabulary

| Term | Student-Friendly Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Vulnerable** | likely to become endangered if threats continue | A species declining because habitat is shrinking |
| **Endangered** | at high risk of extinction | A species with very low numbers |
| **Critically endangered** | at extremely high risk of extinction | A species close to disappearing in the wild |
| **Extinction** | complete disappearance of a species | No living members remain |

## Main Concept Explanation

Threatened species are grouped by risk level. Philippine examples include species such as the Philippine eagle, tamaraw, and Philippine crocodile, whose populations are affected by habitat loss, hunting, and pollution.

The important study move is to connect the vocabulary to a clear relationship. Ask yourself: **What parts are involved? What changes or stays the same? What evidence would show that the idea is correct?**

## Misconception Alerts

- Endangered and extinct are not the same; endangered species still exist but are at high risk.
- Do not classify a species by popularity; use reliable conservation information.
