- Speed, direction, and motion can be described using words, numbers, arrows, and diagrams.
- Simple circuits need a source, conductors, and a complete path.

## Main Concept Explanation

Higher-energy EM waves such as ultraviolet, X-rays, and gamma rays can damage living cells. UV overexposure can cause sunburn, eye injury, and increased skin-cancer risk. X-rays are useful in medicine but exposure is limited because large doses can damage tissues. Gamma rays are very energetic and require careful shielding.

### Learning Objectives

- Explain why higher-energy EM waves can harm living cells.
- Describe harmful effects of UV, X-rays, and gamma rays.
- Identify protective actions such as sunscreen and shielding.
- Interpret risk as depending on energy, intensity, and exposure time.

## Misconception Alerts

- **Common mistake:** Low-frequency waves are not automatically dangerous like gamma rays, but strong exposure to some lower-energy waves can still cause heating. Risk depends on type, intensity, and time.
- Do not confuse a description with an explanation. A description says what happens; an explanation says why it happens.
- Check whether forces act on the same object or on different objects before deciding if they cancel.

## Error Analysis

A learner says: “The biggest arrow or most energetic wave is always the correct answer.” This is incomplete. A good Physics explanation must match the **specific situation**. Ask: What object or wave is being studied? What quantity changed? What evidence supports the answer?
