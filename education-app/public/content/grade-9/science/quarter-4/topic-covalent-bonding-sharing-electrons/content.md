## Main Concept Explanation

### Idea 1: A covalent bond forms when atoms share valence electrons

A covalent bond forms when atoms share valence electrons.

### Idea 2: Covalent bonding usually happens between nonmetal atoms

Covalent bonding usually happens between nonmetal atoms.

### Idea 3: In water, oxygen shares electrons with two hydrogen atoms, forming an H₂O molecule

In water, oxygen shares electrons with two hydrogen atoms, forming an H₂O molecule.

### Idea 4: In carbon dioxide, carbon shares electrons with two oxygen atoms

In carbon dioxide, carbon shares electrons with two oxygen atoms. The molecule is CO₂.

### Idea 5: Covalent substances often exist as individual molecules rather than ionic lattices

Covalent substances often exist as individual molecules rather than ionic lattices.

## Misconception Alerts

- **Trap:** A molecule is not the same as an ionic crystal lattice.
- **Trap:** Subscripts count atoms in the molecule; they are not charges.
