# What Is Biotechnology?

> [!GOAL] Learning Goal
>
> By the end of this lesson, you should be able to recognize biotechnology as the use of living organisms, cells, or biological processes to make useful products or solve practical problems.

## Start Here

Bread, yogurt, vaccines, insulin, composting, and some improved crops all involve using biology for human purposes.

**Estimated time:** 45-60 minutes  
**Difficulty:** Grade 10 Life Science  
**Materials:** notebook, pen, and the lesson diagram

## Key Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Biotechnology** | use of organisms, cells, or biological processes for a purpose | yeast making bread rise |
| **Microorganism** | microscopic living thing | yeast or bacteria |
| **Product** | useful output of a process | medicine, food, enzyme |
| **Traditional biotechnology** | older methods using organisms without gene editing | fermentation |
| **Modern biotechnology** | newer methods using cells, DNA, or reproductive technologies | insulin made by modified bacteria |

## Core Explanation

- Biotechnology is not only modern gene editing. Humans have used living systems for food and farming for thousands of years.
- Modern biotechnology often works at the level of cells, DNA, enzymes, or embryos.
- A good biotechnology example should identify the organism or biological process, the product, and the problem it helps solve.

> [!IMPORTANT] Core Idea
>
> Biotechnology is not only modern gene editing. Humans have used living systems for food and farming for thousands of years.

## Worked Example: Yeast in bread making

1. Yeast is a living microorganism.
2. It uses sugar and releases carbon dioxide gas.
3. Gas bubbles make dough rise.
4. Humans use the process to make bread texture lighter.

**What this shows:** Biotechnology uses a biological process for a practical product.

## Compare The Cases

| Case | What happens | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Food | fermentation | bread, yogurt, vinegar |
| Medicine | cells or microbes make products | insulin, vaccines |
| Agriculture | improve crops or animals | disease-resistant crops |
| Environment | organisms break down waste | composting, bioremediation |

> [!WARNING] Common Trap
>
> Do not limit biotechnology to GMOs. Fermentation is also biotechnology.
