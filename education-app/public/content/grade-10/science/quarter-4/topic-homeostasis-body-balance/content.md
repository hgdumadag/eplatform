## Main Concept Explanation

Homeostasis is the ability of an organism to keep internal conditions within a healthy range even when the outside environment changes.

Key points to remember:

- Homeostasis is not complete stillness; it is active regulation within a safe range.
- Body systems cooperate: the nervous system detects change, the endocrine system sends chemical signals, and organs carry out responses.
- Common indicators include body temperature, blood glucose, blood pressure, water balance, and breathing rate.

The important study move is to connect the vocabulary word to a process. Instead of asking only “What is the definition?”, ask: **What causes it, what changes, and what evidence would show that it happened?**

## Misconception Alerts

- Homeostasis does not mean one exact number forever. It means staying within a healthy range.
- Do not memorize an example without understanding why it fits the concept.
- Do not treat one observation as final proof; compare it with the full context.
