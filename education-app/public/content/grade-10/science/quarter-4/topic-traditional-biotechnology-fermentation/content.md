## Main Concept Explanation

Fermentation is a traditional biotechnology process where microorganisms change sugars or other materials into useful products.

Key points to remember:

- Fermentation changes raw materials using microorganisms or their enzymes.
- Many cultures, including Filipino food traditions, use fermentation.
- Fermentation can preserve food, create flavors, and produce useful substances.
- Clean handling matters because not all microbial growth is safe.

The important study move is to connect the vocabulary word to a process. Instead of asking only “What is the definition?”, ask: **What causes it, what changes, and what evidence would show that it happened?**

## Misconception Alerts

- Fermentation is controlled microbial activity; it is not the same as unsafe food spoilage.
- Do not memorize an example without understanding why it fits the concept.
- Do not treat one observation as final proof; compare it with the full context.
