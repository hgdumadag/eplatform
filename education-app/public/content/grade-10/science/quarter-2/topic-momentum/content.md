## Key Vocabulary

- **momentum** — quantity of motion, equal to mass times velocity
- **mass** — amount of matter in an object
- **velocity** — speed in a direction
- **impact** — effect of one object striking another
- **unit** — a standard measurement such as kg·m/s

## Main Concept Explanation

- Momentum equals mass × velocity.
- A heavy object and a fast object both can have large momentum.
- At the same speed, the object with greater mass has greater momentum.
- At the same mass, the object with greater velocity has greater momentum.

These ideas come from the Grade 10 Quarter 2 MATATAG physics module. Focus on relationships: what changes, what stays the same, and what evidence would support your explanation.

## Misconception Alerts

- Momentum is not the same as speed; mass also matters.
- Do not treat formulas or diagrams as decorations. They are tools for explaining relationships.
- If two situations look similar, compare the variables carefully before deciding they have the same outcome.
