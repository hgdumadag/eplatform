## Key Vocabulary

- **impulse** — force applied over a time interval
- **force** — a push or pull
- **time interval** — duration over which a force acts
- **change in momentum** — difference between final and initial momentum
- **peak force** — largest force during an impact

## Main Concept Explanation

- Changing momentum requires force acting during a time interval.
- A larger force or a longer time can produce a larger change in momentum.
- For the same change in momentum, increasing stopping time lowers the average force.
- This idea explains many safety designs.

These ideas come from the Grade 10 Quarter 2 MATATAG physics module. Focus on relationships: what changes, what stays the same, and what evidence would support your explanation.

## Misconception Alerts

- Longer stopping time does not mean the object stops less; it means the same stop can happen with less force.
- Do not treat formulas or diagrams as decorations. They are tools for explaining relationships.
- If two situations look similar, compare the variables carefully before deciding they have the same outcome.
