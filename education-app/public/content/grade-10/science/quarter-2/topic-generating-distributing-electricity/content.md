## Key Vocabulary

- **generation** — production of electrical energy
- **transmission** — long-distance movement of high-voltage electricity
- **distribution** — delivery of electricity at safer voltages to users
- **substation** — facility that changes voltage levels
- **electric meter** — device that measures household electricity use

## Main Concept Explanation

- Electricity delivery has three major stages: generation, transmission, and distribution.
- High voltage is used for long-distance transmission to reduce energy loss.
- Substations step voltage down for safer local distribution.
- Meters measure electrical energy used by homes and businesses.

These ideas come from the Grade 10 Quarter 2 MATATAG physics module. Focus on relationships: what changes, what stays the same, and what evidence would support your explanation.
