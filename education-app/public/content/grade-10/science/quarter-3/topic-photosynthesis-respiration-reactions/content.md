## What You Should Already Know

Before starting, recall these ideas:

- A **chemical reaction** changes reactants into products.
- Reactants are written on the left side of an equation. Products are written on the right side.
- The arrow means "yields" or "produces."
- Matter is conserved in reactions. Atoms are rearranged, not created from nothing.
- Plants are producers because they can make glucose using light energy.

## Main Concept Explanation

### 1. Photosynthesis stores energy

Photosynthesis happens in plants, algae, and some bacteria. In plants, it takes place mainly in leaf cells that contain chlorophyll.

**Word equation:**

`carbon dioxide + water + light energy -> glucose + oxygen`

**Formula equation:**

`6CO2 + 6H2O + light energy -> C6H12O6 + 6O2`

The carbon atoms from carbon dioxide become part of glucose. Hydrogen and oxygen atoms from water are rearranged. Light energy is not a substance, but it is required to drive the reaction. The result is glucose, a molecule that stores energy in chemical bonds.

### 2. Cellular respiration releases energy

Cellular respiration happens in plant and animal cells. It breaks down glucose using oxygen so the cell can do work, such as growth, repair, movement, transport, and maintaining body processes.

**Word equation:**

`glucose + oxygen -> carbon dioxide + water + energy`

**Formula equation:**

`C6H12O6 + 6O2 -> 6CO2 + 6H2O + energy`

Respiration is not the same as breathing, although breathing helps supply oxygen and remove carbon dioxide in many animals. Cellular respiration is the chemical process inside cells.

### 3. The reactions are connected

Photosynthesis and respiration are often described as reverse processes because the products of one are the reactants of the other.

| Substance | In photosynthesis | In respiration | Cycle role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CO2` | Reactant | Product | Moves carbon from air to glucose and back to air |
| `H2O` | Reactant | Product | Supplies atoms for glucose formation and is reformed in respiration |
| `C6H12O6` | Product | Reactant | Stores energy captured from light |
| `O2` | Product | Reactant | Supports energy release from glucose |

### 4. Matter cycles, energy flows

The same kinds of atoms appear on both sides when the formula equations are balanced. This shows conservation of matter.

Energy behaves differently. It does not cycle in the same simple way:

- Photosynthesis captures light energy and stores it in glucose.
- Respiration releases energy from glucose for cell use.
- Some energy eventually spreads to the surroundings as heat.

## Key Idea Box

> [!IMPORTANT] Key Idea
>
> Photosynthesis stores energy by making glucose from carbon dioxide and water. Cellular respiration releases energy by breaking glucose down with oxygen. Together, they link living things, food energy, and atmospheric gases.

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Identify reactants and products

For photosynthesis:

`6CO2 + 6H2O + light energy -> C6H12O6 + 6O2`

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Reactants | `CO2`, `H2O`, and light energy |
| Products | `C6H12O6` and `O2` |
| Energy change | Energy is stored in glucose |

### Example 2: Trace one carbon atom

Suppose a carbon atom starts in atmospheric carbon dioxide.

1. During photosynthesis, that carbon atom can become part of glucose.
2. A plant may use that glucose for growth or respiration.
3. If respiration breaks down the glucose, the carbon atom can return to the air as carbon dioxide.

This is a chemical cycle. The carbon atom is not destroyed; it is rearranged into different molecules.

### Example 3: Compare energy storage and release

Question: Which reaction gives a cell immediate usable energy?

Answer: **Cellular respiration**, because it releases energy from glucose. Photosynthesis makes glucose, which is a stored-energy molecule.

## Misconception Alerts

> [!WARNING] Common Traps
>
> **Trap 1:** "Plants only photosynthesize." Plants also perform cellular respiration because plant cells need usable energy.
>
> **Trap 2:** "Respiration means breathing." Breathing is gas exchange. Cellular respiration is a chemical reaction inside cells.
>
> **Trap 3:** "Energy is a product molecule." Energy is released or absorbed, but it is not a material substance like oxygen or glucose.
>
> **Trap 4:** "Photosynthesis creates matter from sunlight." Sunlight provides energy. The atoms in glucose come mainly from carbon dioxide and water.

## Error Analysis

A student writes:

`oxygen + glucose -> carbon dioxide + light energy`

The student says this is photosynthesis.

What is wrong?

- The reactants `oxygen + glucose` match cellular respiration, not photosynthesis.
- Respiration releases usable energy, but it does not produce light energy as a main product.
- Photosynthesis should begin with carbon dioxide, water, and light energy.

Correct photosynthesis word equation:

`carbon dioxide + water + light energy -> glucose + oxygen`

Correct respiration word equation:

`glucose + oxygen -> carbon dioxide + water + energy`

## Final Summary

Photosynthesis and cellular respiration are chemical reactions that connect life to matter and energy. Photosynthesis uses carbon dioxide, water, and light energy to make glucose and oxygen. Cellular respiration uses glucose and oxygen to produce carbon dioxide, water, and usable energy. The products of one reaction help supply the reactants of the other, creating a cycle of gases and molecules between organisms and the environment.
