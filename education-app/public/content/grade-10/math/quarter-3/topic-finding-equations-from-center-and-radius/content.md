## What You Should Already Know

Before writing circle equations, make sure these ideas feel familiar:

- A point on the coordinate plane is written as `(x, y)`.
- The center of a circle is a fixed point `(h, k)`.
- The radius is the distance from the center to any point on the circle.
- Squaring means multiplying a number by itself.
- Parentheses matter when a coordinate is negative.

## Key Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Circle | The set of all points the same distance from one fixed point |
| Center | The fixed point in the middle of the circle |
| Radius | The distance from the center to any point on the circle |
| Center-radius form | `(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2` |
| `h` | The x-coordinate of the center |
| `k` | The y-coordinate of the center |
| `r` | The radius |
| `r^2` | The squared radius, which appears on the right side of the equation |

> [!IMPORTANT] Core Idea
>
> If the center is `(h, k)` and the radius is `r`, then the circle equation is:
>
> $$(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$$

## Main Concept Explanation

### 1. Start With the Template

The center-radius form of a circle is:

$$ (x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2 $$

The center is `(h, k)`. The radius is `r`.

![Center-radius equation template](/content/grade-10/math/quarter-3/topic-finding-equations-from-center-and-radius/images/circle-equation-template.svg)

### 2. Substitute the Center Carefully

If the center is `(4, 2)`, then `h = 4` and `k = 2`.

The left side becomes:

$$ (x - 4)^2 + (y - 2)^2 $$

If the center is `(-3, 5)`, then `h = -3` and `k = 5`.

The x-part becomes:

$$ x - (-3) = x + 3 $$

So the equation begins:

$$ (x + 3)^2 + (y - 5)^2 $$

> [!WARNING] Common Sign Trap
>
> The formula is `(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2`. If the center coordinate is negative, subtracting it creates a plus sign.

### 3. Square the Radius

The right side is not `r`. It is `r^2`.

![Radius squared check](/content/grade-10/math/quarter-3/topic-finding-equations-from-center-and-radius/images/radius-squared-check.svg)

If `r = 6`, then:

$$ r^2 = 36 $$

If `r = \sqrt{13}`, then:

$$ r^2 = 13 $$

## Rule Box / Formula Box

| Given | Equation part |
|---|---|
| Center `(h, k)` | `(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2` |
| Radius `r` | Right side is `r^2` |
| Center `(0, 0)` | `x^2 + y^2 = r^2` |
| Center `(a, -b)` | `(x - a)^2 + (y + b)^2 = r^2` |

## Worked Example

### Example: Write the Equation

**Problem:** Write the equation of a circle with center `(2, -3)` and radius `5`.

**Step 1: Identify h, k, and r.**

Center `(h, k) = (2, -3)`, so `h = 2` and `k = -3`.

Radius `r = 5`.

**Step 2: Substitute into the template.**

$$ (x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2 $$

$$ (x - 2)^2 + (y - (-3))^2 = 5^2 $$

**Step 3: Simplify.**

$$ (x - 2)^2 + (y + 3)^2 = 25 $$

**Answer:** `(x - 2)^2 + (y + 3)^2 = 25`

## Misconception Alerts

| Misconception | Correction |
|---|---|
| Writing the radius instead of the squared radius | The right side is always `r^2`, not `r`. |
| Changing the signs without checking the formula | Use `(x - h)` and `(y - k)` first, then simplify. |
| Treating `(x + 4)^2` as center `4` | `(x + 4)^2` means `x - (-4)`, so the x-coordinate is `-4`. |
| Forgetting parentheses | The squared binomial must stay grouped, such as `(y + 3)^2`. |

## Error Analysis

A learner writes this equation for a circle with center `(-5, 2)` and radius `6`:

$$ (x - 5)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 6 $$

What mistakes do you see?

<details>
<summary>Reveal mistake explanation</summary>

There are two mistakes.

First, the center has x-coordinate `-5`, so the x-part should be `(x + 5)^2`, not `(x - 5)^2`.

Second, the radius is `6`, so the right side should be `6^2 = 36`, not `6`.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Reveal corrected solution</summary>

The correct equation is:

$$ (x + 5)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 36 $$
</details>

## Extension Challenge

A circle has center `(4, -1)` and passes through the point `(4, 5)`. Write its equation.

<details>
<summary>Hint</summary>

The center and the point have the same x-coordinate, so the radius is the vertical distance between `-1` and `5`.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Reveal solution</summary>

The radius is `6`, because the distance from `y = -1` to `y = 5` is `6`.

So:

$$ (x - 4)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 36 $$
</details>
