3. Try Before Reveal

A playground is an 18 m by 11 m rectangle with a semicircle attached to one 11 m side.

Find the total area. Then find the outside border length.

Reveal: Rectangle area is 18 x 11 = 198. Semicircle radius is 5.5, so area is 1/2 x 3.14 x 5.5² = 47.49. Total area is 245.49 m². Border length is 18 + 18 + 11 + half circumference. Half circumference is 1/2 x 3.14 x 11 = 17.27. Total border is 64.27 m.
