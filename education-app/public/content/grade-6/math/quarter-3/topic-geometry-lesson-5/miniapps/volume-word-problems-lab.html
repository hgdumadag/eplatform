Guided Word Problem

A toy box is 20 cm long, 12 cm wide, and 10 cm high. Find its volume.

The toy box is a rectangular prism.
Use 20 × 12 × 10, then include cm³.
V = 20 × 12 × 10 = 2400 cm³. The toy box has a volume of 2400 cm³.
