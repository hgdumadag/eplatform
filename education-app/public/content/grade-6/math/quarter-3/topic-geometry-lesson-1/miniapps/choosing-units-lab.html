Which object should use cubic units, and which should use liquid units?

Ask whether the question is about 3D solid space or the amount of liquid a container holds.
The box uses cubic units such as cm³. The bottle uses liquid units such as mL or L.

Guided Unit Matching

Solid 3D spaces need cubic units. Liquid capacity needs mL or L.
Small solids often use cm³. Large rooms often use m³. Small liquid containers often use mL.
Dice: cm³. Medicine cup: mL. Classroom: m³.
